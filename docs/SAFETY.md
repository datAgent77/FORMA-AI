# Phase 1 safety and privacy constraints
- Before starting or resuming, require the user to confirm a clear space and visible real surroundings. Keep the Quest system boundary enabled. Clear other people, pets and obstacles. No room geometry or safe-space detection is implemented.
- Use small, comfortable movements, stay in place, keep eyes open, and stop for discomfort, dizziness or visibility loss. The prototype is not medical advice or a rehabilitation program. Suitability must be assessed individually before a supervised trial.
- Passthrough remains active during exercise, rest, pause, history and exit confirmation. Never enter an opaque world as a fallback. Provider failure prevents start/resume and pauses active exercise. The application cannot force system passthrough to work after a runtime failure; users must stop and use the headset system controls if the physical environment is no longer visible.
- A provider's initialization flag and layer activation are not a direct measurement of rendered visibility, latency or image quality. The user's confirmation and real-device tests remain required. No boundary collision prediction is claimed.
- B/Y pauses independently of gaze targeting. A large on-screen pause button also exists. Resuming requires a fresh clear-space confirmation and explicit Resume. Exit pauses first and requires a second action. Ending a workout does not require confirmation because stopping should be immediate.
- The first experience requires Touch controllers; hand-only controls, voice, audio cues and accessibility adaptation are not implemented. Validate button reachability and gaze usability before allowing unsupervised exercise.
- No heavy weights, external equipment, jumping, deep squats or fast lunges. Load fields are self-reported notes only and do not alter the equipment-free workout. The simple counter cycles 0–100 repetitions and 0–5 kg in 0.5 kg steps; wrap-to-zero is a prototype editing control, not a training prescription.
- Neither the coach animation nor system tracking can establish exercise form quality. There is no automatic rep counting, calorie estimation, weight inference or precise equipment localization. Manual entries are labeled as such.
- No Worlds/Mirror transition exists. A later immersive transition must be disallowed while equipment is handled and must not rely on unvalidated equipment detection to assert it is safe.
- Session/profile data stays in the application sandbox. No camera frames, raw motion logs, voice recordings, AI requests or telemetry are generated by project code. Review SDK defaults/manifest/network activity on device; local processing does not imply SDK telemetry has been audited. Debug/developer access can expose plaintext JSON. Delete local data from the side panel; prevent accidental deletion during active sessions.

Release must remain blocked until the hardware checklist is completed on both target headsets. This document describes engineering safeguards, not certification that exercise is safe.
